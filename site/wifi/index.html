DUSTBOY KIT · POST-FLASH

Wi-Fi setup

Configure the monitor only after the USB flash has completed and the board has rebooted.

Fallback access point

  1. Power-cycle the monitor and wait up to 90 seconds.
  2. Join Wi-Fi network DBK-xxx with password 12345678.
  3. Open http://192.168.4.1 and save your network.

The AP remains active for approximately 15 minutes. Once connected, use http://dbk-001.local/ (or the hostname shown by your router).

USB / Improv

If the firmware exposes Improv Serial, use a Chromium browser after the device has settled. Do not chain Wi-Fi provisioning immediately after flashing: USB re-enumeration must finish first.
